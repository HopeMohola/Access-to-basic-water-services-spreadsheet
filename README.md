# Global Access to Drinking Water Analysis

This project dives into the global issue of clean and affordable drinking water. Using data, we explored trends, disparities, and actionable insights to help address one of the world’s most critical challenges: access to clean water for all. This aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 6: *Clean Water and Sanitation*.

---

## **What’s This Project About?**

We wanted to find answers to questions like:
1. Who has access to clean water and who doesn’t?
2. What regions are lagging behind?
3. How can we use data to guide better decisions?

By cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing the data, we uncovered insights that can inform meaningful actions.

---

- **Google Sheets** for data wrangling and analysis.

### **Key Skills Applied:**

---

## **Key Findings**

Here’s what we learned:

1. **Who’s Leading and Who’s Lagging?**
   - High-income countries are close to 100% clean water access.
   - Sub-Saharan Africa is struggling, and current progress indicates universal access may not be reached until 2080.

2. **Urban vs. Rural Reality:**
   - Urban areas tend to have better access to basic water services.
   - Rural areas, however, are catching up at a faster pace.

3. **Income Matters:**
   - Low-income regions have higher reliance on unimproved and surface water.
   - High-income regions enjoy nearly universal basic water access.

---

## **How We Solved Challenges**

- **Mismatch Between Dataset and Global Benchmarks:** We calculated percentage differences to highlight gaps.
- **Finding Countries with 100% Access:** Used smart filters and logic to isolate universal access cases.
- **Comparing Access Across Income Groups:** Built pivot tables to summarize and visualize disparities.
